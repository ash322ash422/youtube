Loxford Academy — Financial Aid FAQ

Q: How do I apply for financial aid?
A: Complete the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) at studentaid.gov. 

Q: What types of financial aid are available?
A: We offer federal grants (Pell Grant, SEOG), institutional scholarships, work-study positions, and federal student loans (Direct Subsidized and Unsubsidized). Merit scholarships are awarded automatically based on your admission application.

Q: When will I receive my financial aid award letter?
A: Award letters are sent within 2-3 weeks of your FAFSA being processed. Most students receive their letters by mid-June for the following academic year.
